Mutant flowers 2025
In 2015, mutated daisies photographed near Fukushima began circulating online. Images of the flowers became vehicles for fake news and visual speculation. In the age of generative images, however, these same photographs are increasingly dismissed as fabricated – the real image starts to function like a hallucination.
The book revolves around the gesture of holding a flower, photography, evidence, and generation. The AI-generated daisies are based on documented plant mutations, while the hand gestures recreate motifs found in photographs from internet forums. These are interwoven with the artist's collages made on living plants and with digital interventions into photographic images.
After spending six months generating daisies with AI, the artist unexpectedly discovers a real mutated daisy in Poland.
